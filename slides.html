Dev advocacy plans at LiveKit (draft #1)

The big picture (for shared understanding)

  • Real-time audio and video are hard: latency, reconnection, scale
  • Demand is exploding: ChatGPT today, plus xAI, Salesforce, Spotify, healthcare, and more
  • Then video and physical AI: robotics and sensor data are the same kind of streams
  • LiveKit solves this on both sides: open source stack + operated global infra (100ms global latency, 99.99% uptime)

General theme

Become the expert in public: here's how

  • Create content to answer my own questions
  • Talk to customers and potential customers
  • Learn and improve what exists: docs, blog posts, materials
  • Build deep expertise in real-time audio, video, and sensor data
  • Use that expertise to speak, answer questions, and host events confidently

Start in San Francisco and expand

  • Present and record talks in San Francisco
  • Turn each talk into a video, a blog post, a Reddit post, etc.
  • Use different formats to reach different audiences, both people and AI
  • This is exactly what I did at Sourcegraph: luma.com/sourcegraph

30 / 90 / 360 day plans

  • 30 days: learn as much as possible, improve existing material, join existing events
  • 90 days: set the direction, try multiple approaches and measure: hackathons, speaking at events, hosting our own events, etc.
  • 360 days: double down on what works, follow the data, scale the most effective strategies, build the flywheel: customers → case studies → talks → videos / blog posts → even more customers
  • Logistics: based in Vancouver, travel to San Francisco every two to four weeks
